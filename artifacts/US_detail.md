# User Stories Detail (MVP - FitAI)

US1: As a user, I want to sign up and log in to access my account. Actors: User, AuthService. Flows: email/password signup, login, password reset. Acceptance: user is authenticated and profile created.

US2: As a user, I want a quick onboarding (3-5 min) to input objective, age, gender, height, weight, experience level, constraints. Acceptance: onboarding completes and profile saved.

US3: As a user, I want to see initial training & diet plan after onboarding. Acceptance: plan generated by IA and visible in Home.

US4: As a user, I want to log workouts and meals with timestamps, types, quantities, calories/macros. Acceptance: logs stored and queryable.

US5: As a user, I want progress charts (weight, BF%, measurements, PRs) and weekly photos. Acceptance: visuals render and data updates weekly.

US6: As a user, IA adjusts weekly plan with simple rules (deficit, macros, training volume) with justification. Acceptance: rules applied and justification present.

US7: As a user, I receive notifications for workouts, meals, check-ins. Acceptance: notifications delivered per settings.

US8: As an admin/coach, I can create and adjust IA rules and review usage logs. Acceptance: admin actions tracked.

US9: As a user, I can ask basic questions about training/diet and receive answers based on history. Acceptance: IA responds with relevant guidance.
